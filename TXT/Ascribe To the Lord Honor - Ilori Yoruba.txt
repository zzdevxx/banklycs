Ascribe To the Lord Honor - Ilori Yoruba

VERSE 1
Ascribe to the Lord, honor, And glory due His Name Worship in His holiness, Worthy is His pow'r and fame.

VERSE 2
The Lord of glory thunders O'er great and mighty seas; His voice is so majestic, It breaks cedar to pieces.

VERSE 3
His voice like a mighty flash, Shakes the desert of Kadesh, It makes bare the thick forest No other voice is its match.

VERSE 4
The Lord sits upon His throne, That's set upon the flood. He gives strength to His people, Who put their trust in His word.

VERSE 5
Ascribe to the Lord, honor, And glory due His Name, Worship Him in holiness Worthy is His might and fame.
