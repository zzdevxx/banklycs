What Child Is This - Greensleeves - Charlotte Church

VERSE 1
What child is this, who, laid to rest
On Mary's lap, is sleeping?

Whom angels greet with voices sweet
While shepherds watch are keeping?.

CHORUS 1
This, this is Christ the King
Whom shepherds guard and angels sing

Haste, haste to bring Him praise
The Babe, the Son of Mary!

VERSE 2
So bring Him incense, gold, and mercy
Come peasant king to praise Him

The King of king, the glory brings
Let loving hearts enthrone Him.

CHORUS 2
Raise, raise the song on high
Let singing fill the earth and sky

Joy, joy, for Christ is born
The Babe, the Son of Mary!
