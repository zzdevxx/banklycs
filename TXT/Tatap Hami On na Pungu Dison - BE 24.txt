Tatap Hami On na Pungu Dison - BE 24

VERSE 1
Tatap hami on, na pungu dison:
Ai na naeng pujionnami

denggan ni basaM di hami.
Tatap hami on, na pungu dison.

VERSE 2
Togu hami on, na mardosa on,
Ale Jesus, Tuhannami

sai asi rohaM di hami!
Togu hami on, na mardosa on.

VERSE 3
Sai ramoti be hami on sude,
Maradophon musunami,

asa unang mago hami,
Sai ramoti be hami on sude.

VERSE 4
Sai urasi be hami on sude
Asa muba rohanami

jala ias tondinami.
Sai urasi be hami on sude.

VERSE 5
Sai ajari be hami on sude.
Asa lam huboto hami,

dalan sidalanannami
Sai ajari be hami on sude.

VERSE 6
Sai pintori be hami on sude
molo unduk rohanami,

manopoti dosanami.
Sai pintori be hami on sude.

VERSE 7
Sai padungo be hami on sude
Unang loas modom hami

di bagasan dosanami.
Sai padungo be hami on sude.

VERSE 8
Sai pamasuk be hami on sude
Di na jumpang ajalnami,

molo suda bohalnami
tu ingananMi, hasonangan i.
