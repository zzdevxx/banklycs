Taiti Gogo Ihut tu ho - BE 101

VERSE 1
Taiti gogo ihut tu Ho, palungun rohanami
Tu surgo i ingananMi, o Jesus Tuhannami.

VERSE 2
Togu tongtong ma hami on, di dalanMi tu surgo.
Anggo so i oloanMi, sai saut do hami mago

VERSE 3
Alai tahe suraon be, so oloanMu hami
PinorsanMi do dosa i, lao paluahon hami.

VERSE 4
Dibahen i, o Jesuski, sai naeng ihuthononku
Di hataMi, nang langkaMi, saleleng au mangolu

VERSE 5
Taiti gogo ihut tu Ho au tu banua ginjang
Asa disi pujionki, denggan basaM o Tuhan.
