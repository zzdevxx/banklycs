Great And Mighty - True Worshippers

VERSE 1
I will sing
of the mercies of the Lord

With my mouth
will I sing of Your great love.

VERSE 2
I will lift
my voice to nations end

Crying out
Who is mighty like our God.

BRIDGE
For everything is Yours
the Heaven and the earth

The north east and west
Let every tongue confess

and every knees shall bow
Let all the nations sing.

CHORUS
Great and mighty are You Lord
Who was and is to come

Righteous ruler of the earth
Who reigns forever more.
