Dung Salpu Borngin - BE 816

VERSE 1
Dung salpu borngin ro buha ijuk,
Rap dohot Jesus sonang do au,

Nunga mansohot sude na porsuk
Dung salpu borngin ro na tiur.

VERSE 2
Dung salpu borngin ditogu ahu,
Manopot Ama parholong i

Jonok Tuhanku manghaol au;
Dung salpu borngin lao biarhi.

VERSE 3
Dung salpu borngin tiur sudena,
Maporus ombun, galumbang i

Marpos ni roha do saluhutna;
Dung salpu borngin las roha i.

VERSE 4
Dung salpu borngin pajumpang hita,
Dohot donganta naung monang i

Di sambulonta ndang be marsirang;
Dung salpu borngin martua i.
