O Hamuna na Porsea - BE 240

VERSE 1
O hamuna na porsea, sai tong hamu mangalo jea, utian ni pangago i
Hata ni Debatamuna, ma baen hamu sinjatamuna, ndang talu be hamu disi.

Bisuk pe musu i, ditanda rohami, jou ma Jesus
Sai monang do baenonna ho, alai musunta talu do.

VERSE 2
Sai paias rohamuna Huhut sai alo hisapmuna ai halak Kristen do hamu
Sai papos ma rohamuna, di Tuhan Jesus Rajamuna ai urupanNa do hamu

Naeng suda pe gogom, sai tatap sambulom, na di ginjang
Sonang disi tondinta i, dung sahat hita ro disi.

VERSE 3
Ale Jesus pargogoi, ma nasa jolma sioloi, hatam di hasiangan on
Paimbaru rohanami, huhut papir ma tondinami, ai na gale do hami on

Tu rohanami be, pasaor ma TondiMi, na badia
Sai burju be di Ho sude, mangolu manang mate pe.
