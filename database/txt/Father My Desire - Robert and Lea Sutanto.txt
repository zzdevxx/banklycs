Father My Desire - Robert and Lea Sutanto

VERSE 1
Father my desire
Is to please Your heart

Teach me Lord to know You
In a deeper way.

VERSE 2
This one thing I ask
Make me worthy Lord

Take me into the most holy place
To worship You behind the veil.

CHORUS
Coming to meet You face to face
Emerge into Your love eternal One

Oh My Father I long to worship You
And dwell in Your Holy place.
