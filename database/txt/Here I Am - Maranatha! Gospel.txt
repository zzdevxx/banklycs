Here I Am - Maranatha! Gospel

VERSE 1
If You asked me for an offering
I'd give it

If You wanted sacrifice
I would obey

But my life is all
I have and I will live it

Dedicated to the honor of Your Name.

Gladly will I bow down
As Your servant

Gladly will I wait for your command
I commit my trust to You

For You deserve it
I delight within the guidance

of Your hand.

in my heart

I've lifted up Your name

I've told the congregation, Lord
Of Your great salvation, Lord.
