Psalm 103 - JPCC Worship (2)

VERSE 1
My whole heart, my whole life
Bow in wonder of Your love

How could I ever forget
Your grace is miracle to me

VERSE 2
I boast nothing but my Christ
Not my power, not my wisdom

I will trust You with my heart
I will wait here in Your presence

VERSE 3
Whom have I in heaven
I desire none but You

Though my flesh, they may fail
My portion You are all I need

VERSE 4
Your greatness, Your power
Your love is washing over me

Amazing Your grace is
Now I know that I am free
