Beta sai Taendehon ma - BE 438

VERSE 1
Beta sai taendehon ma Tuhanta Jesus i
Hosana diusehon do mangkophop jolma i

Mudar ni Tuhan i do hangoluanki
Tung dame jala las do rohangki

Tung mudar ni Tuhanku do mamuri rohangki
Marolopolop tondingki marningot asimi

O hatuaon i, na so boi salpui
Humophop au do mate Tuhanki.

VERSE 2

O ho na marsak i, sai ro tu Tuhanmi
Nang ho boi malum dibaen Jesus i

Tung mudar ni Tuhanku do mamuri rohangki
Marolopolop tondingki marningot asimi

O hatuaon i, na so boi salpui
Humophop au do mate Tuhanki.
