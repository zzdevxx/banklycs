Hohom ma ho - BE 735

VERSE 1
Hohom ma ho, hohom ale tondingku
Satongkin taon ma parungkilonmi,

Tu Tuhan I pasahat ma holsomu
Iluilum nang dohot lungunmi

Haposi ma huaso ni Tuhanmu
Daging tondim sai ramotanNa i.

VERSE 2
Hohom ma ho, hohom ale tondingku,
Ai Jesus do tongtong partogimi,

Marungkil ho di tingki naung salpu
Tingki na ro gok las ni rohami,

Ndang lonong ho nang gumalunsang laut,
Ai Debata do donganmi disi.

VERSE 3
Hohom ma ho, hohom ale tondingku,
Paborhat ma hinaholonganmi,

Ponjot roham marabur iluilu,
Na sai laon rosu parsaoranmi,

Pos ma roham di Tuhan Debatamu,
Na margogoi mangapul rohami.

VERSE 4
Hohom ma ho, hohom ale tondingku,
Satongkinnai boi ma pajumpang ho,

Pos rohami satia do Tuhanmu,
Tu surgo I do boanonNa ho,

Raphon sude na hinaholonganmu,
Dohot suruan i marende ho.
