I'll fly away, O glory - Worship Hymn

VERSE 1
Some glad morning
when this life is over,

I'll fly away.
To a home on God's

celestial shore
I'll fly away.

CHORUS
Kor: I'll fly away, oh glory,
I'll fly away.

When it's time
Halleluja by and by,

I'll fly away.

VERSE 2
When the shadows of
this life have grown,

I'll fly away.
Like a bird from prison

bars has flown,
I'll fly away.

VERSE 3
Just a few more weary
days and then,

I'll fly away.
To a home where joy

shall never end,
I'll fly away.
