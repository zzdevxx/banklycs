VERSE 1
Your steadfast love,
extends to the heavens

Your faithfulness,
it reaches to the clouds

Your righteousness,
is like majestic mountains

And Your wisdom's like
a depth on the sea

and You come to me.

with Your loving kindness

I find my peace,

from the abundance of Your household

And I drink from the stream of rejoicing
You are my King.
