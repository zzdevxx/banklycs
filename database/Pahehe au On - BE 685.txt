VERSE 1
Pahehe au on ale Debatangki,
ala Jesus naung hehe mangolu tongtong.

VERSE 2
Haleluya puji Tuhan, haleluya amen,
Haleluya puji Tuhan, pahehe au on.

VERSE 3
Pahehe au on Tondi ni Tuhanki,
Na papatar tu hami Sipalua i.

VERSE 4
Pahehe au on Jesus sihophop au,
Pola mate tarsilang Ho manobus au.

VERSE 5
Pahehe au on, sai patau ma au on,
Baen panondang tu dongan di na rodop on.
